hackernews · dsr12 · Oct 23, 19:09 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50213925)

**「Background」** Most voice assistants are turn-taking systems: they stop listening while they speak, so interruptions and overlapping speech must be simulated rather than handled natively. Full-duplex, or bidirectional, speech models keep input and output streams open at once, which is the mechanism behind talking over the user, back-channeling, and lower response latency. The local-inference claim also lands against a moving hardware baseline: Apple&\#x27;s current MacBook Pro line uses M5, M5 Pro, and M5 Max chips introduced in 2026, making the M4 Max named by Block a previous-generation part.

**「Deployment considerations」** For teams weighing a local voice agent, Dessa&\#x27;s stated hardware floor — a MacBook Pro M4 Max or better — limits on-device use to high-end Apple silicon and excludes phones and lower-end Macs. The announcement supplies no independent latency measurements or benchmark scores, and full-duplex overlap handling is still under-evaluated by external suites such as Full-Duplex-Bench v1.5, so developers should test interruption and turn-taking behavior on their own audio before committing to the model.

**「Community Discussion」** Commenters praised the technical ambition, particularly the claim that Dessa can run locally on a high-end Mac and be trained on one GB200 NVL72. Others criticized the demos as artificial, citing forced interruptions and laughter, and questioned the use of “most authentic ever” as a marketing claim.

### [UUIDv7 misconceptions: ordering, indexing, and identifier semantics](https://alexsci.com/blog/uuidv7-is-weird/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

A technical blog post argues that common assumptions about UUIDv7, particularly strict chronological ordering and database-indexing behavior, are misplaced. The Hacker News discussion treats the post as a debate about what UUIDv7 is actually for, rather than as a new specification or release.

hackernews · ibobev · Oct 23, 16:39 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50211746)

**「Background」** UUIDv7 is a time-ordered UUID variant intended to preserve some of the index-locality benefits of sequential integer keys while still allowing identifiers to be generated independently across clients. Its timestamp component keeps UUIDs created around the same time close together in a database index, but the format does not by itself guarantee strict creation-order sorting or make the identifier suitable for exposure as a public ID.

**「Impact」** Python developers adopting UUIDv7 should verify library compliance: a commenter reports that the PyPI \`uuid7\` package implements a noncompliant draft format and recommends \`uuid-utils\` instead.

**「Community Discussion」** Commenters dispute the article&\#x27;s framing, with ricardobeat and kentm arguing that UUIDv7 is designed for write clustering and time-range locality across shards, not strict chronological ordering. ricardobeat also says UUIDv7 should not be used as a public identifier.

The advisory says only applications that switch the non-default serialize\_handler to “php” at runtime may be affected. The foundation had previously recommended keeping upload progress disabled because of CVE-2026-109619. The source post did not include a fixed PHP version or patch details.

telegram · zaihuapd · Oct 23, 13:46

**「Background」** session.upload\_progress is PHP&\#x27;s built-in feature that writes upload progress data into $\_SESSION; an advisory summary notes that it is enabled by default. The reported exposure requires a runtime switch of session.serialize\_handler to &quot;php&quot; while upload progress is enabled, according to a separate mitigation write-up. The source adds that the PHP Foundation had previously recommended leaving the feature disabled in connection with CVE-2026-109619.

**「Operational impact」** Operators should set session.upload\_progress.enabled=0 now, since the flaw also affects current fully patched releases such as PHP 8.5.12 and no fixed build exists yet; upload progress functionality will be unavailable until the next regular bugfix release ships. Re-enabling should wait for that release and its full advisory, and applications that switch session.serialize\_handler to &quot;php&quot; at runtime before session\_start are the configurations that actually need the mitigation.
